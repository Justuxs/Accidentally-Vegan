02 · Why we exist

Vegan doesn't always look vegan.

A lot of the food you already reach for contains no animal-derived ingredients at all — it's just never marketed that way. No loud claims, no green boxes, no long explanations on the label.

Accidentally Vegan exists to make those products easy to spot, so choosing plant-based can be less of a project and more of a… “wait, I already buy this?”

  • No habit overhaul needed — just a sharper glance at the shelf.
  • Ordinary stores, everyday products, zero special ordering.
  • Discovery over persuasion — curiosity does the talking.
